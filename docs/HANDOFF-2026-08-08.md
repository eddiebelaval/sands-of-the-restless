## THE TWO PATTERNS THAT CAUGHT THE MOST BUGS TONIGHT

**1. Measure the thing the player gets.** The ceiling design was REVERSED by a
measurement: a dark night-sky soffit lost 69.2% of a gold scarab's body. The
same trap caught the frieze's sunk recess at 55.3%. Twice, the physically obvious
dark choice would have eaten the beetle. No amount of code review finds that.

**2. Never sample on a stopwatch.** Six separate assertions across three files
were written as fixed-time samples, failed, and had to be rewritten to wait for
state. Every one of them was measuring the machine. If a check reads a value
`N` milliseconds after an action, it is probably wrong.
